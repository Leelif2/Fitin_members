03 / ADDING WEIGHT

Reps first, then weight

Double progression: keep the weight until every set hits the top of the rep range, then add weight.

Example: bench press, 3 sets × 6–8 reps

Same weight each session — add a rep here and there.

  1. Session 16/6/6
  2. Session 27/6/6
  3. Session 37/7/6
  4. Done!8/8/8

Once every set hits 8, add weight next session.

How much to add

  • Upper body: add 1–2.5 kg
  • Lower body: add 2.5–5 kg
  • Form slipping or anything hurts? Don’t add weight.

    Safety first

    Form comes before weight. Stop right away if you feel joint pain or any sharp pain. For the squat, bench press and deadlift, have your form checked early on — by a trainer, an experienced lifter, or by filming yourself.
