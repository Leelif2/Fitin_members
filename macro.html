How it’s calculated

    Limits and cautions

    Of the formulas compared, Mifflin-St Jeor is the most likely to land within ±10% of measured values, but some people still see large errors. It was built on data from ages 19–78, so don’t use it outside that range, while pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you have a medical condition or take medication — talk to a doctor or dietitian instead. If you carry a lot of muscle the formula may underestimate your energy use, which makes the two-week correction above especially important. 7,700 kcal/kg is an approximation, and your energy use drops as you lose weight, so recalculate every 2–4 weeks.
